Privacy policy

Web Education upload is a private tool used only by the owner of the Web Education YouTube channel, to upload that channel's own films.

  • It asks Google for access to manage the signed-in YouTube channel, and uses that access only to upload videos, captions and thumbnails and to add videos to the channel's playlists.
  • It reads no other data from the Google account and collects nothing about viewers or other people.
  • The only thing it stores is the sign-in token, kept encrypted in the owner's private repository. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
